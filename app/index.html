Black Friday Deal Finder

Real Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals, each with its store, its sale price, its usual price, and an honest verdict on whether the discount is really a deal.

How the verdict works: “New low” means the price beats every price the deals sources recorded before; “Strong price” clears the 30% peak-discount benchmark; “Good price” is a real cut that is not a record; “Modest discount” is a small cut on something rarely discounted.

Prices move during the sale. Check the store before you buy.
